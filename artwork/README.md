Elegant bold modern sans serif wordmark, highly legible, centered below the symbol. Symbol: a beautifully simple bold turquoise bicycle with two clear wheels and an intelligible frame, above it a golden yellow sun partially behind a small white cloud, two small turquoise rain marks hinting at weather forecast. Flat vector-like professional raster logo, crisp geometric forms, strong silhouette, minimal detail, no gradients, no shadows, no mockup. Uniform solid dark navy #0B2335 background filling the entire square to every edge, no rounded corners or border. All symbol and wordmark artwork must fit within the central 72 percent of the square width and height, with generous empty navy margin on every edge. Harmonious stacked compact composition, weather above bicycle, wordmark underneath. No other text, no watermark, no old branding. This will be used as a launcher icon, Play Store icon and in-app brand image.
